Three-Chamber Technology

01 • Primary Catalytic Chamber

Converts CO and hydrocarbons to CO₂ and H₂O through oxidation using platinum, palladium, and rhodium catalysts.

CO + O₂ → CO₂

02 • Secondary NOx Chamber

Uses Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) with urea to break down NOx into nitrogen and oxygen.

2NO + 2NH₃ + ½O₂ → 2N₂ + 3H₂O

03 • CO₂ Conversion Chamber

Employs molten carbonate electrolysis at 700°C to convert CO₂ into solid carbon and oxygen gas.

CO₂ + 4e⁻ → C + 2O²⁻

Carbon Storage System

The system produces approximately 30.5kg of solid carbon per 1000km of travel. Our automated fan-based collection mechanism uses mesh filters and weight sensors to capture and store carbon efficiently. With a 37kg storage capacity, the system provides approximately 1200km of travel between maintenance intervals.
